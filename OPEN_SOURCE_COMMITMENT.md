# Rastok — Open Source Commitment

This document describes the commitments the Rastok project makes about
the open-source nature of its runtime and language.

## What is open source

**Rastok** — the runtime, the DSL compiler, the standard library
of nodes, the simulation harness, and the reference graphs in this
repository — is released under the **[Apache License 2.0](LICENSE)**.

This license grants anyone the perpetual right to:

- use the software for any purpose, including commercial use;
- modify the software;
- redistribute the software, modified or not;
- combine the software with proprietary code;
- create derivative works;

without paying any royalty, signing any contract, or asking permission.

## What stays open source, forever

We make the following explicit commitments:

1. **Every release of Rastok that has been published under the
   Apache License 2.0 will remain available under the Apache License
   2.0, forever.** Past releases cannot and will not be retroactively
   relicensed.

2. **Any proposed change to the license of Rastok will go through
   a public RFC** with a minimum notice period of **twelve (12)
   months** before taking effect. The community will have full
   visibility and opportunity to fork before any new terms apply.

3. **We will not use the Rastok trademark to restrict the kinds of uses
   that the Apache License 2.0 permits.** Specifically, the trademark
   policy will not prohibit forks, compatible implementations, plugins,
   integrations, benchmarks, tutorials, consulting services, or
   commercial hosting of Rastok-compatible workloads. See
   [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

4. **We will not weaponize patents against users or contributors of
   Rastok.** The Apache License 2.0 already includes a strong
   patent grant; we will not undermine it.

## Why this commitment

Infrastructure users need to know which rights are granted by the
license and which rules only protect the project's identity. Apache 2.0
governs the software. The trademark policy prevents confusion about
origin or endorsement without narrowing the license. No future
commercial roadmap is implied by this document.

## How to verify

This document, [LICENSE](LICENSE), and [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md)
are all under version control. Any change to them is visible in the
git history. If you depend on these commitments, you can pin a
specific commit and verify that future commits do not silently change
the terms.
