# Code of Conduct

Rastok welcomes direct technical disagreement. Critique a design,
implementation, claim, or patch as rigorously as needed; do not attack,
humiliate, or harass the person behind it.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence, invariants, and trade-offs in good faith;
- give actionable criticism and accept clear negative decisions;
- respect differences in experience, identity, language, and background;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, and
  publication of another person's private information;
- stop an interaction when a maintainer asks for a cooling-off period.

This applies in project repositories, review threads, community spaces, and
public events where someone is representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close discussions, restrict
participation, or ban a participant when necessary to protect the project and
its contributors. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and may be
reconsidered when new evidence appears.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact address published in
the maintainer's profile on the canonical repository. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive personal information.
